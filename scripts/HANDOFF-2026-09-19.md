## The four open rows

Full text in `openddil-contracts/decisions/FOLLOW-UPS.md`.

1. **`hq_link_severed` tracks the simulator, not reachability.** It probes

## Things that will bite

1. **Do not `helm upgrade` mid-session — two reasons now.** UD-14, and the
   wipe hook, which fires on every upgrade and covers **all four** Restates.
   Safe on the lab (state is rebuildable), wrong on camera. Also the reason
   `ephemeralOnUpgrade` must stay false anywhere intent custody will live.
2. **After any bulk store correction, restart that tier's Electric.** Electric
   serves from an append-only log: deleting rows *appends* operations, so a
   cleanup can make the served shape **bigger**. Measured: a table went 11 MB
   → 64 kB while its shape stayed at 10 MB until Electric restarted.
   `check-shape-sizes.sh` is the only instrument that sees this.
3. **Log in to all four screens before the first cut** — Keycloak is at the
   root; a severed tier cannot mint sessions.
4. **Predict counts and classifications before any cut or delete.** This paid
   twice this week: a stated pre-count caught a wrong "5 real rows" (it was
   11) before a delete, and classification-first is what made the severance
   rehearsal a test rather than a demo.

## Method notes worth keeping

These are the recurring shapes, and every one cost real time:

* **A column that answers a different question.** `RESTARTS` counts lifetime
  restarts, not restarts since an event — caught before it became "nine
  restarts during the upgrade."
* **An embedded language is validated by its host, not itself.** A comment
  inside a line continuation made a whole Job script unparseable; YAML, helm
  template, lint, apply and image-pull all said yes. Guard 4 now parses every
  rendered shell script with a real shell.
* **A mechanism covering one of N is an absent mechanism with a reassuring
  name.** The Restate wipe hook covered 1 StatefulSet of 4.
* **A second implementation of a rule is a second rule.** The gate's naming
  query disagreed with its own counting rule and accused three correct rows.
* **Red-check against a mutation that models the original defect** — and
  check *why* it went red. One red-check "passed" on an `ImportError`, which
  proves an import is missing, not that the defect is caught.
* **Absence of evidence buys nothing.** The `sparse` category fails closed
  when nothing has measured the producer recently.
