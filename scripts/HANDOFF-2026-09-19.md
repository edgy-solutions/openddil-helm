## Method notes worth keeping

These are the recurring shapes, and every one cost real time:

* **A column that answers a different question.** `RESTARTS` counts lifetime
  restarts, not restarts since an event — caught before it became "nine
  restarts during the upgrade."
* **An embedded language is validated by its host, not itself.** A comment
  inside a line continuation made a whole Job script unparseable; YAML, helm
  template, lint, apply and image-pull all said yes. Guard 4 now parses every
  rendered shell script with a real shell.
* **A mechanism covering one of N is an absent mechanism with a reassuring
  name.** The Restate wipe hook covered 1 StatefulSet of 4.
* **A second implementation of a rule is a second rule.** The gate's naming
  query disagreed with its own counting rule and accused three correct rows.
* **Red-check against a mutation that models the original defect** — and
  check *why* it went red. One red-check "passed" on an `ImportError`, which
  proves an import is missing, not that the defect is caught.
* **Absence of evidence buys nothing.** The `sparse` category fails closed
  when nothing has measured the producer recently.
