DDIL sever (toxiproxy):
  Each tier's own WAN toggle severs that tier's own uplink through
  /proxies/uplink (toxiproxy uplink-<id>, to its parent broker). The root
  (HQ) toggle severs HQ's own uplink, /proxies/hq-link, the advertised
  address of the HQ broker's PLAINTEXT listener. Disabling a proxy severs
  the link for real, not simulated.
